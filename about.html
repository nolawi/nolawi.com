About Nolawi.

I’m Nolawi Petros. I build front-end systems, help teams make the web more accessible, and spend a fair amount of my free time looking after my cabin.

What I do

I came to engineering through graphic design. I still like that moment when a visual idea becomes a working interface. These days I spend a lot of time on the parts teams use over and over: components, style guides, and the rules that help different apps feel like they belong together.

At AbbVie I led accessibility work across Adobe Experience Manager sites and helped bring testing into the release process. At the SEC, through Pyramid Systems, I built a shared design system and worked with engineers across applications to put it to use. At Everforth I supported CISA. Earlier, at National Geographic Society, I helped turn design patterns into React components and a style guide. My toolkit includes semantic HTML, CSS, TypeScript, Angular, React, and Vue. I care just as much about how an interface behaves with a keyboard as how it looks on the screen.

Connecting applications to AI

I’m a Lead UI Developer at Tarkik Solutions, where I work on AI integrations for government applications.

In my government work I connect applications to language models. I care about what happens between a question and an answer: how the app finds the right data, what the person is allowed to see, and whether the answer makes it easy to double-check the source. Read how I think about adding chat to an app.

Outside work

I’m an amateur architect. I like sketching spaces, designing furniture, painting, and making things with my hands. Looking after my cabin gives me plenty of chances to figure out how things fit together and how to fix them. I also ride my bike, get out in a kayak, and spend time with my dog.
