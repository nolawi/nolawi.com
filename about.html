About Nolawi.

I’m Nolawi Petros. I build front-end systems, help teams make the web more accessible, and spend a fair amount of my free time looking after a log cabin.

What I do

I came to engineering through graphic design. I still like that moment when a visual idea becomes a working interface. These days I spend a lot of time on the parts teams use over and over: components, style guides, and the rules that help different apps feel like they belong together.

At AbbVie I led accessibility work across Adobe Experience Manager sites and helped bring testing into the release process. At the SEC, through Pyramid Systems, I built a shared design system and worked with engineers across applications to put it to use. At ECS Federal my work included CISA. Earlier, at National Geographic Society, I helped turn design patterns into React components and a style guide. My toolkit includes semantic HTML, CSS, TypeScript, Angular, React, and Vue. I care just as much about how an interface behaves with a keyboard as how it looks on the screen.

Building with AI in mind

When an AI coding tool helps write a component, I want it to have the same context I’d give a teammate: which patterns to reuse, how the component should behave, and what it needs to pass before it ships. A design system is a good home for that context. Read how I’d put that into practice with a dialog.

Outside work

Taking care of a log cabin means getting to know how it was put together, figuring out what needs attention, and steadily learning how to fix things. I like making things with my hands, riding my bike, getting out in a kayak, painting, and spending time with my dog.
