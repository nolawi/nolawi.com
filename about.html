About me.

I still get a kick out of turning an idea into a page you can actually use. I like working out how it should feel when you read it, move through it, or come back to it later.

What I like about the web

I like the small decisions that make a page feel right. Where does focus go when you close something? Is the text still easy to read when you make it bigger? Can you tell what happened after you press a button? I enjoy getting those details right and turning them into components other people can build with.

That’s what got me into design systems. I like seeing a pattern work on one screen, then figuring out what has to change for it to work on ten. I’m interested in the same questions when an app gets a chat interface. What can you do with the answer? Can you get to the record it came from?

Off the clock

I’m an amateur architect. I like sketching spaces, designing furniture, painting, and making things with my hands. Looking after my cabin gives me plenty of chances to figure out how things fit together and how to fix them. I also ride my bike, get out in a kayak, and spend time with my dog.
