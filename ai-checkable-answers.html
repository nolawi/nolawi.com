AI integration · User experience

Make the source part of the answer.

If I ask an app which requests are open, I want to see the rows it found. Five lines of confident prose still leave me with work to do if I can’t get to the records.

Give me somewhere to go

Say I’m checking open service requests in the North region. A useful reply shows the region, the status, and the matching IDs. I can open a record and pick up the work there. If it turns out I meant South, I can change the filter without starting over.

The table and the conversation should agree about what they found. If a reply says two records matched, I should be able to open both. If there’s a limit or a filter I didn’t ask for, make it visible in the interface.

A reply can say it doesn’t know

Then I ask why one of those requests is still open. The record may show a status without giving a reason. In that case I’d show the row and say the reason isn’t recorded. That’s a handoff back to the app and the people who know the work.

Try it without a mouse

I’d test the whole trip by keyboard. Can I ask the question, find the new reply, open the source, and return to the conversation? Each step should be easy to find and should have a clear place for focus.
